# Code of conduct

ADB Connector is a small project, and everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests or
discussions — is expected to keep it friendly and useful.

**Please do**
- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to ADB or Android.
- Keep to the topic, and give enough detail for others to help.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project.

**Not accepted**
- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Offensive or sexual language or images.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Spam or advertising.

**What happens**
Comments that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeated or serious cases can
lead to being blocked from the project. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile ([@angreenade](https://github.com/angreenade)).
