# Security

## Reporting a problem
If you find a security issue in ADB Connector, please report it privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/angreenade/adb-connector/security/advisories/new)
instead of opening a public issue. You'll get a reply as soon as possible.

## What the app does with your data
- Everything runs on your computer. The app has no account, no analytics and sends no data anywhere.
- The only internet connection it makes is to check GitHub Releases for app updates.
- Saved devices, settings, logs and your app library stay in your Windows user folder
  (`%APPDATA%\ADB Connector`) and are kept when you update or uninstall.
- ADB gives full control of a connected device. Only pair devices you own, and turn off
  Wireless debugging on the device when you're done.
